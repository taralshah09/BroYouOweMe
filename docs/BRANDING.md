# BroYouOweMe logo

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The receipt buddy uses the app's cobalt, cream, peach and navy palette. Transparent PNG artwork is centered at the top of the README; favicon and Apple touch variants are linked from the shared Django base template.

## Assets

- `static/branding/logo.png`: original 1254 x 1254 transparent artwork.
- `static/branding/favicon.ico`: 16, 32, 48 and 64 pixel icon sizes.
- `static/branding/favicon-32.png`: 32 pixel browser icon.
- `static/branding/icon-192.png`: 192 pixel browser icon.
- `static/branding/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 pixel touch icon.

PNG and ICO variants were resized from the original with Pillow using Lanczos resampling. The original generated artwork is preserved.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished standalone app logo for BroYouOweMe, a friendly shared-expense app whose tagline is 'Split the bill. Keep the friendship.' Asset: square app icon usable at 16px favicon and 120px GitHub README. Primary visual: a bold, memorable little receipt buddy: warm cream receipt silhouette with a folded peach top-right corner, two simple expressive navy eyes and a small confident friendly smile. The bottom of the receipt has just three broad zigzag teeth suggesting a split bill. Place the cream receipt centrally on a rich cobalt-blue (#3548d6) rounded-square tile with generous curved corners. Slight jaunty 7-degree tilt of the receipt, not the tile, to feel social and human. Simple extremely clean flat vector-like graphic, thick confident silhouette, optically balanced, professional indie app brand. Cream #fff0bd, peach #ff976c, navy #202b40, blue #3548d6. Tile fills about 88 percent of square canvas; transparent outside tile. No typography, no currency signs, no lettering, no border, no drop shadow, no 3D, no gradients, no tiny receipt lines, no floating decorative shapes, no mockup, no variants sheet. One centered finished symbol. Actual transparent background.
